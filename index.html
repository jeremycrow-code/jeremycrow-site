Gnostic Luciferian Alchemy & Esoteric Architecture

Jeremy Crow (Tau Cerinthus III) is a Canadian esoteric educator, author, Gnostic Bishop, and community architect with over 40 years of experience in lucid dreaming and more than 30 years of hands-on practice across Western mystery traditions. His work centers on Gnostic Luciferian Alchemy—a non-dogmatic synthesis of Ophite gnosis, Western laboratory and Eastern internal alchemy, esoteric chivalry, and lucid mastery of consciousness.

Trained across classical initiatory currents beginning in 1999, Crow holds valid tactile apostolic succession as an independent, autocephalous Gnostic Bishop (Tau Cerinthus III, succeeding to the Canadian See of Cerinthus following his mentor +Carl Emmanuel, Tau Cerinthus II). He is a 3° Supérieur Inconnu (S∴I∴) in the Continental Martinist current, holds documented Templar chivalric tactus from his October 13, 2000 knighthood under Patriarch Ronald V. Cappello, and received direct operational alchemy lineage descending from Frater Albertus through Rubaphilos Salfluere. He has also held officer and initiatory roles in Co-Masonry, the Rite of Memphis-Misraïm, the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, and the O.T.O.

After leading and open-sourcing the original Ordo Luciferi (2006–2010), founding the Luciferian Research Society (2008), launching the Flambeau Noir International Left Hand Path Conference (2012), inaugurating Year Zero of the New Luciferian Era (Winter Solstice 2012), and serving as early president and co-president of the Greater Church of Lucifer / Assembly of Light Bearers, Crow established the Novus Ordo Luciferi (NOL) on December 21, 2023. Structured as a co-creative mystery school and research laboratory, the NOL encompasses an Outer Order (The Crystal and The Crystal Court) alongside four specialized Inner Orders: the Sub-Lunary Triad, the Infernal Arcana, the Knights of the Ziggurat (chartered November 4, 2026), and the Gnostic Temple of the Morning Star.
